Our Story

A family-owned mom-and-pop cafe in historic Downtown Matthews, North Carolina.

Our Roots

Family-Owned in Downtown Matthews

Royal Cafe & Creperie is a family-owned cafe in historic Downtown Matthews. Client materials identify Alexis and Elena as the founding mother-and-daughter team, with Alexis Mizrahi-Botero listed as the owner/operator.

Located at 131 Matthews Station Street, Suite 1-E, the cafe serves sweet and savory crepes, locally roasted Charlotte coffee, and loose-leaf teas.

What We Serve

Crepes, Local Coffee & Loose-Leaf Tea

The menu includes sweet and savory crepes, with gluten-free and vegan options available.

The cafe also serves coffee roasted locally in Charlotte and loose-leaf teas.

We are open everyday from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM at 131 Matthews Station Street, Suite 1-E.
