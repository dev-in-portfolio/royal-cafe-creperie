Our Story & Community

A family-owned mom-and-pop cafe in historic Downtown Matthews, North Carolina.

Our Roots

Family-Owned in Downtown Matthews

Royal Cafe & Creperie was founded by the mother-and-daughter duo of Elena and Alexis. Owned and operated by Alexis Mizrahi-Botero, Royal Cafe was created to bring comforting food, friendly hospitality, and an inviting home-away-from-home feeling to the Matthews community.

Located on Matthews Station Street in historic Downtown Matthews, the cafe serves a welcoming, diverse community of neighbors, regulars, and visitors every day.

What We Care About

Crepes, Local Coffee & Community Care

We offer a variety of sweet and savory crepes, with gluten-free and vegan options available so everyone can find something to enjoy.

We are proud to support local small businesses, serving specialty coffee roasted locally right here in Charlotte alongside a wide selection of loose-leaf teas.
