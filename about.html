Our Story & Philosophy

A family-owned cafe born out of a passion for scratch cooking, quality ingredients, and warm neighborhood community in Downtown Matthews.

Our Roots

How Royal Cafe Began

Royal Cafe & Creperie was founded by the mother-and-daughter duo of Elena and Alexis. It was their dream to share the comfort foods they grew up loving, prepared with fresh, high-quality ingredients and modern American twists.

Located on Matthews Station Street in the heart of historic Downtown Matthews, Royal Cafe was built to be a warm, welcoming space where guests can gather over fresh-spun crepes, artisanal coffees, and relaxing conversation.

Kitchen Standards

Scratch Batters & Dietary Care

Our kitchen focuses on using high-quality ingredients sourced locally whenever possible. We prepare our signature crepe batters fresh from scratch every morning.

Recognizing that dietary needs vary across our community, we are proud to offer specialized Gluten-Free and Vegan scratch batter options. We believe that everyone should be able to sit at our tables and enjoy a freshly made meal.

Local Connections

Locally Roasted Coffee & Community Gathering

We are more than just a creperie — we are a neighborhood coffee stop. We proudly serve specialty coffees roasted right here in Charlotte using quality beans from around the world, alongside an extensive menu of premium loose-leaf green, black, and herbal teas.

Whether you are meeting coworkers for a morning beverage, catching up with friends over weekend brunch, or visiting with family, we are honored to be a part of your daily routine.
