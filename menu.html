Gluten-Free & Vegan Friendly

Dietary Accommodations & Custom Crepes

We believe everyone should get to experience our crepes! We proudly offer specialized Gluten-Free and Vegan scratch batters. You are also welcome to create your own custom crepe from our wide selection of fresh toppings and fillings.

🥞 Sweet Crepes (Crêpes Sucrées)

Scratch Batters • GF & Vegan Available

Delicate, golden crepes filled and topped with premium dessert spreads, freshly sliced seasonal fruits, and sweet glazes.

The Royal Trinity Guest Favorite

Our signature dessert combination: rich Nutella spread, freshly sliced strawberries, and ripe bananas folded into a warm crepe.

Banana & Peanut Butter Glaze

Creamy peanut butter, fresh banana slices, organic honey or sweet syrup, and toasted nuts for a rich, comforting treat.

Classic Lemon Sugar & Sweet Cream

Freshly squeezed lemon juice, powdered confectioner's sugar, and sweet butter syrup for a bright, classic profile.

Build Your Own Sweet Crepe

Choose your batter (Standard, Gluten-Free, or Vegan) and select your choice of fresh berries, chocolate, fruit spreads, and sweet toppings.

🍳 Savory Crepes (Crêpes Salées)

Breakfast & Lunch Folds

Hearty breakfast and lunch creations folded fresh on our griddles with premium cheeses, proteins, and garden-fresh vegetables.

Roasted Chicken & Basil Pesto

Tender chicken breast, melted whole milk mozzarella, diced fresh tomatoes, and aromatic basil pesto folded hot.

Fresh Spinach & Feta Fold

Fresh baby spinach leaves, crumbled feta cheese, roasted seasonings, and light savory dressings.

Morning Breakfast Crepes

Scrambled egg, cheddar cheese, breakfast meats, and diced tomatoes for a satisfying morning start.

Build Your Own Savory Crepe

Pick your batter and combine your favorite meats, artisanal cheeses, and vegetables for a custom savory meal.

☕ Locally Sourced Coffee & Teas

Charlotte Roasted Beans

Specialty coffees roasted right here in Charlotte, handcrafted espresso drinks, and fine loose-leaf teas brewed hot or iced.

Specialty Espresso Drinks

Double shots, Americanos, cappuccinos, and handcrafted lattes with your choice of dairy or plant-based milks.

Charlotte House Drip Coffee & Cold Brew

Freshly ground and brewed local roasts, served hot or as chilled cold brew.

Premium Loose-Leaf Teas

Curated selection of loose-leaf green, black, and herbal teas steeped fresh to order.
