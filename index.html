Our Roots

Family-Owned Hospitality

Founded by mother and daughter Elena and Alexis, Royal Cafe & Creperie was created out of a passion for sharing comfort foods made from scratch with modern American touches and authentic neighborhood warmth.

Whether you are stopping by for a quiet morning espresso, meeting friends for brunch, or picking up treats for the family, our doors are open daily.
